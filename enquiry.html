Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, payment and collection.

Please enquire at least 5 business days before collection for birthday and baby shower cakes, and at least 14 days ahead for a wedding cake so we can arrange a tasting and consultation.

Yes, we can make eggless, dairy free and nut free options on request. Our kitchen does handle nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs every day, so we cannot promise a completely allergen free environment.

Once we have confirmed the design and price, we ask for a 50% deposit by EFT. The balance is paid when you collect your order.

You can collect from either our Melville or Greenside store. Tell us which one suits you and we will confirm it with your quote. Opening times and directions are on the contact page.

We deliver within Johannesburg for a fee that depends on the distance and the size of the cake. Delivery is arranged once your order is confirmed.
